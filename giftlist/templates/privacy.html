{{ app_name }} is a small, private gift list for one family. This page covers what happens to your information.

What we collect. When you sign in with Google, we store your name, email address, and the wishlist items and claims you add. That's it — no analytics, no trackers, no ad networks.

How it's used. Solely to run the gift list: showing your items to others, showing you what's been claimed on lists you're allowed to see, and letting the admin manage the family's accounts.

Who sees it. Only people the site admin has invited — this isn't a public app. Your own household never sees who has claimed your items.

Sharing. We don't sell or share your data with anyone outside this app.

Deletion. Ask the admin to remove your account and your list and claims are deleted with it.

Contact. Questions? Ask whoever sent you the invite link.
